In brief

I coordinate strategic, capital, and market planning across the seven health care systems that make up VISN 17, the VA network serving Veterans throughout Texas.

I started at VA as an unpaid volunteer in December 2022.

That progression is the most useful thing about how I work. Before the network office I was scheduling volunteers in North Little Rock, and before that I spent twelve years in the Army keeping missile guidance systems running. I have seen this organization from the volunteer desk, from a training billet, and from the office where multi-year capacity decisions get made — which is why I am skeptical of any plan that has not been checked against whether the people at the bottom of it can actually execute.

Day to day the work is demand and capacity: demographic mapping, workload analysis, space needs assessments, bed control, and the business case analyses that turn a projected gap into a funded plan.
