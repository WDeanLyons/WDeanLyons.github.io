### Entry 2 — 2024–2025 · GHATP · VISN 17 · ACHE presentation

**Expanding ketamine administration routes for treatment-resistant depression**

The network operated an esketamine nasal spray clinic. Nasal esketamine carries REMS requirements, observation time, and cost characteristics that limit how many Veterans a clinic can serve, while intravenous and intramuscular racemic ketamine offer a different operational and cost profile for the same clinical population.

I analyzed expanding the existing clinic's administration routes to include IV and IM racemic ketamine, and presented the findings to the American College of Healthcare Executives.
